Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Content Publisher ("we", "our"), an internal tool used at EuroJET, handles your data.

1. Information We Access

Our application interacts with the TikTok API and YouTube API solely to automate video uploads to our authorized company accounts. We specifically request the video.upload and video.publish scopes for TikTok, and the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope for YouTube to transfer locally processed video files directly to the platforms. We do not access or collect unrelated personal profile data.

2. Data Usage & Storage

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. API access tokens are stored securely on our private server infrastructure and are used exclusively to communicate with official social media platform APIs for publishing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

This service operates using official TikTok and YouTube APIs.

YouTube API Services: We use YouTube API Services to enable direct video uploads. By using this application, you are agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. In addition to our internal data deletion procedures, users can revoke Content Publisher's access to their Google account data at any time via the Google Security Settings page.

TikTok API: By using this tool to upload to TikTok, you also comply with TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
